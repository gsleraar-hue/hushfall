Visuals

Web Audio does all of its work on a single processor core, and the room reverb on each sound is by far the most expensive part of that. Light mode drops that reverb and halves the particles: things sound a little drier, but there is room to spare when your PC is busy. The reverb change applies to sounds you start after switching.
